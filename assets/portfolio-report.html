Key wins

What was found and acted on this quarter

1 June - 15 September 2026.

12 Harbour Lane (NSW)

Reliability

Condenser water pump CWP-02 returned to duty after a seized bearing

LevelB2

CWP-02 tripped on overload three times in July and the standby pump carried the load alone. Kestrel Air Services found a seized motor bearing, replaced the bearing set and realigned the coupling on 4 August, and the pump has run its duty cycle without a trip since.

CWP-02 - Inspect Pump Overload Trip

Parkview Centre (NSW)

Energy

Out-of-hours operation stopped on AHU-03

LevelL3

AHU-03 was running through weekends on a holiday schedule left in place after a tenant fitout. Linwood Controls restored the standard schedule on 19 August, with an estimated saving of $4,200 a year.

AHU-03 - Operating Out of Hours

Notes on method

How these numbers are calculated

  1. Portfolio rows. Scores are weighted by building size; counts are summed.
  2. Scored equipment. Equip and Rules count equipment and rules that scored in the period, so they can sit below the monitored totals in the scorecards.
  3. Completion rate. Open now includes actions raised before the quarter.
  4. Sustainability ratings. The portfolio row counts current ratings only, and its arrow compares the same sites' previous ratings.
  5. The reporting window. September 2026 covers 15 of 30 days, to the issue date. Actions resolved this quarter include those days; scores are unaffected.
