Key wins

Faults found by continuous monitoring and fixed on the floor this quarter, with recovery verified in PEAK.

Chiller 2 returned to stable duty — condenser water valve replaced

Chiller 2 had been cycling against a hunting condenser water valve since early June, dragging chiller plant health from 96.9% to 94.7%. Our technicians replaced the failed valve actuator on 3 July, and PEAK verified the fault clear across four weeks of stable operation since. The chiller plant carries the building's cooling into the warmer months, so this is the repair that matters most on this page.

Also visible in the equipment health snapshot: Chiller, up 0.25 pp and recovering.

Action #48213 · Action #48307

Levels 4–6 comfort restored — shared VAV control template corrected

Five VAV boxes across levels 4–6 sat in fault on the same airflow rule: a shared control template was holding minimum airflow below design, starving the zones on warm afternoons. We corrected the template, pushed it to the affected boxes on 26 June, and PEAK verified recovery on every one. Tenants on those floors get stable temperatures back, and the fans stop working against a bad setpoint.

Also visible in the equipment health snapshot: Variable Air Volume at 99.03%, back above the Excellent line.

Action #48254 · #48255 · #48256 · #48257 · #48258

Free cooling recovered on AHU-03 — economiser dampers repaired

AHU-03's economiser dampers had seized shut, so the unit was mechanically cooling air that Brisbane mornings could have cooled for free. The damper linkage was freed and re-stroked on 19 June, and the unit now trims mechanical cooling whenever outside air allows. Air handling units posted the site's largest health gain this quarter as a result, up 0.97 pp.

Notes on method

How the modelled and derived figures in this report are calculated.

  1. Labour cost avoided. Each rule replaces a manual inspection at a set frequency by priority — P1 daily at 0.5 minutes per check; P2 weekly, P3 monthly and P4–5 quarterly at 1 minute. Savings prorate that annual effort over the 92-day period, valued at AUD 150/hr for this site's region.
  2. Verified recovery. Recovery rate is alerts resolved in the period with fault status recovered, divided by all alerts resolved in the period, counting alerts with status closed.
  3. Median time to resolve is measured from the linked action's creation to its resolution — not from the alert's own dates, since detection is automatic but resolution is human work.
  4. Raised vs resolved. Raised counts alerts triaged into actions in the month (or 1 where an action has no linked alert); resolved counts the same way on resolution date. Rules must be running; actions marked Not Doing are excluded from both series.
  5. Completion rate is resolved ÷ (resolved + currently open) as at the issue date, so 100% reflects holding no open work.
