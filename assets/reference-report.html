Notes on this report

Every headline figure carries a provenance marker. Three do not yet stand up.

Verified Read directly from PEAK Modelled Derived from stated assumptions Unverified No source located
  1. Thermal comfort 93%, up 3%. No supporting metric was found in PEAK for either the level or the movement. This figure needs a named source and calculation method before the report is issued, or it should be removed.
  2. Building size and thermal zone count. 16,200 m² and 301 zones could not be confirmed through the platform. Both are plausible and likely correct from another system, but they are unsourced here.
  3. Rules running. The site has 1,809 rules deployed, of which 1,712 are running and 1,637 contribute to the health score. The original draft labelled 1,809 as "running", which overstates active coverage by 97 rules.
  4. Equipment health change. The quarter scored 98.79% against 98.33% for the rolling 12 months — a gain of 0.46 percentage points, not 1%. The improvement is real and the trend is consistently upward, but the figure as drafted was roughly double the actual movement.
  5. Labour saving is gross, not net. $18,870 values the manual inspections the rules replace, scaled from the annual assumption model at 92/365 days. It does not subtract the triage effort the alerts generate, and it assumes the replaced inspections were genuinely being performed — a claim that is strongest for the 44 P1 rules and weakest for the 1,511 P3 rules, which account for 61% of the total.
  6. Recovery rate scope. The 100% figure covers the 13 alerts closed on running rules during the quarter. A further 9 closures sat on stopped rules, and 116 alerts across the site currently sit in Recovered status without ever being formally closed. The recovery rate is accurate for what it measures; it is not a measure of overall close-out.
  7. Open actions concentrate on the weakest assets. Five of the nine actions opened on 24 June sit on VAV units that also appear among the site's largest health declines — VAV-04-02, VAV-04-07, VAV-05-08, VAV-06-11 and VAV-18-01. Four of those units score at or near 85.71%, exactly six of seven rules passing, which points to one shared template stuck in fault rather than five separate equipment problems.
