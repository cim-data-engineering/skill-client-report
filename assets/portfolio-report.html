Key wins

Key wins past 90 days

29 July - 26 October 2026.

Fixed

Skyline Tower (QLD)

Reliability

Chilled water flow restored through the CH-3 evaporator

LevelPlantroom

CH-3 kept tripping on low evaporator flow, leaving CH-1 and CH-2 to carry the cooling load through the warmest afternoons. Billy Hargrove from Hawkins Air found the strainer on the CH-3 chilled water circuit two thirds blocked, cleaned it and recalibrated the flow switch on 19 August, and the chiller has run its full sequence without a trip since.

CH-3,CH-3-HLI - Inspect Evaporator Insufficient Flow

Nakatomi Plaza (NSW)

Energy

Overnight running stopped on the comms room unit PAC-Comms-Rm

LevelLevel 04

PAC-Comms-Rm was cooling flat out every night, still held at the 18°C setpoint dropped in for a rack install that had long finished. Orbit Building Services raised it back to 23°C and returned the fan to auto on 6 August, and the unit now cycles overnight instead of running continuously.

PAC-Comms-Rm - Confirm Unit Overnight Operation (Energy Waste)

Starcourt Mall North (NSW)

Reliability

Supply air loss on AHU-L1A3 traced to a split duct connection

LevelMain-L1

AHU-L1A3 was delivering well under its design airflow, and the fan was speeding up to make up for it. Kestrel Air Services found a split flexible connection on the supply duct at the fan outlet and replaced it on 2 September, bringing the unit back to full supply pressure at a lower fan speed, with an estimated saving of $900.

AHU-L1A3 - Inspect Supply Air Loss
